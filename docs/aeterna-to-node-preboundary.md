# AETERNA to Node Pre-Boundary

This document fixes the pre-boundary between AETERNA and any future Node-side structuring layer.

## What AETERNA May Hand Off

AETERNA may hand off pre-semantic observations or candidates such as:

- openness baseline
- stability / volatility
- recovery pressure
- sustained anomaly
- long-cycle coherence shift
- knot candidate
- recurrence locus
- basin candidate
- proto-point candidate

These are field-side observations. They are suitable as material for later structuring, not as completed semantic products.

## What AETERNA Must Not Hand Off as Settled Outputs

AETERNA must not claim the following as ready-made outputs:

- label
- same-object
- category
- natural language interpretation
- teacher verdict

Those belong to a later layer, if they appear at all.

## Boundary Rule

The bridge may expose **patterns, pressures, recurrences, and candidates**. It must not silently upgrade those into semantic certainty while crossing from AETERNA to Node.
