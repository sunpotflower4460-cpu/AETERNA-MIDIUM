# AETERNA Natural Vocabulary

## Geometry / Curvature terms

Allowed observational terms for N1:

- curved torus metric
- major radius / minor radius
- area element
- gaussian curvature
- mean curvature
- tangent / normal
- curvature asymmetry
- inner / outer rim bias as geometry
- observation-only curved mode

## Disallowed claim language

Do not describe curvature as:

- consciousness
- emotion
- soul
- self
- meaning
- mystical center

Curvature remains geometry-derived data and comparison scaffolding for later physics-oriented phases.
