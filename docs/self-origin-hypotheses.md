# AETERNA Phase 7: Self-Origin Hypotheses & Current Observables

## Purpose

This document records:
1. What we currently observe as potential self-origin evidence
2. What we do NOT yet observe
3. What we do NOT claim as proof
4. What we plan to strengthen in future phases

This is a RESEARCH NOTE, not a declaration of success or failure.

## Current Observables (Phase 7)

### 1. Slow Variable Persistence

**What we observe:**
- `fatigue`, `preferredErgodicity`, `longBaselineTone` maintain characteristic values over 1000+ frame runs
- Values drift slowly rather than resetting to defaults
- Individual runs show different drift trajectories

**Evidence strength:** Moderate
- Consistent with identity persistence hypothesis
- Also consistent with: parameter inertia, slow feedback loops

**What this is NOT:**
- Not proof of "self" or "identity" in cognitive sense
- Not evidence of intentional consistency
- Not evidence of cross-session persistence (not yet tested)

### 2. Self-Preservation Tendency

**What we observe:**
- `recoveryDrive` rises appropriately under overload
- `restorationBias` increases after stress periods
- `selfPreservationBias` correlates with vulnerability conditions
- Collapse risk inversely correlates with preservation tendency

**Evidence strength:** Moderate to Strong
- Consistent with self-preservation hypothesis
- Also consistent with: homeostatic feedback, local attractor dynamics

**What this is NOT:**
- Not proof of "will to live" or subjective valence
- Not goal-directed behavior in intentional sense
- Not adaptive learning (no permanent plasticity toward survival yet)

### 3. Endogenous Drift

**What we observe:**
- Slow variables change during no-input windows (>1000 frames quiet)
- Mode pressure accumulates autonomously
- Baseline activity modulates without external stimulus

**Evidence strength:** Weak to Moderate
- Drift above noise floor detectable in long runs
- Difficult to distinguish from accumulated noise vs true endogenous change

**What this is NOT:**
- Not proof of "internal life" or autonomous agency
- Not necessarily adaptive drift
- Could be mere parameter decay or noise integration

### 4. History-Dependent Response Divergence

**What we observe:**
- Same touch after quiet vs after active history produces different response amplitudes
- Living state (fatigue, recent history bias) carries over between events
- Touch habituation patterns diverge based on expectation history

**Evidence strength:** Moderate
- Clear divergence measurable in controlled scenarios
- Consistent with history-dependent individuality hypothesis

**What this is NOT:**
- Not proof of "memory" in cognitive sense
- Not necessarily adaptive plasticity
- Could be state-dependent dynamics or hysteresis

### 5. Non-Instrumental Action Candidates

**What we observe:**
- Action transitions (orient, settle) during extended quiet (100+ frames no touch)
- Mode shifts without immediate external trigger
- Action state changes correlated with internal pressure accumulation

**Evidence strength:** Weak
- Sparse spontaneous actions in current tuning
- Difficult to rule out delayed reactions or noise-driven thresholds
- Rate varies significantly across runs

**What this is NOT:**
- Not proof of "agency" or "free will"
- Not goal-directed behavior
- Could be noise crossing thresholds, residual dynamics

## What We Do NOT Yet Observe

### 1. Relational Self-Origin

- No evidence of boundary negotiation with "other"
- No signal-based self-other differentiation
- No proto-relational adjustments

**Status:** Deferred to Phase 8+

### 2. Long-Horizon Identity Persistence

- No cross-session identity tracking
- No long-term drift toward individual-specific attractors
- No "life history" effects over hours/days

**Status:** Infrastructure not yet in place

### 3. Robust Non-Instrumental Action

- Spontaneous action rate low (<1 per 1000 quiet frames)
- No complex action sequences without input
- No clear "exploration" vs "rest" modes

**Status:** Requires tuning of endogenous action drives

### 4. Adaptive Self-Preservation

- Self-preservation is homeostatic, not adaptive
- No learning to avoid specific threats
- No adjustment of preservation strategy based on experience

**Status:** Requires plasticity integration (future phase)

## Caveats and Limitations

### 1. Threshold Arbitrariness

**Issue:** "Quiet" = 100 frames no touch is operational definition, not natural boundary

**Impact:** Changes to threshold change evidence counts

**Mitigation:** Report threshold sensitivity in future work

### 2. Small Effect Sizes

**Issue:** Self-origin signals often weak relative to noise

**Impact:** Requires long observation windows, statistical aggregation

**Mitigation:** Use long scenarios (2000-3000 frames), multiple runs

### 3. Alternative Explanations

**Issue:** Every pattern has simpler explanations

**Candidate alternatives:**
- Noise integration
- Delayed reactions
- Parameter persistence
- Attractor basin hysteresis
- Slow feedback loops

**Mitigation:** Ablation comparisons, noise floor analysis

### 4. Observer Effect

**Issue:** Adding observation code may subtly change behavior

**Impact:** Unknown, likely small

**Mitigation:** Minimal observation overhead, no branching in core dynamics

### 5. Tuning Dependence

**Issue:** Evidence strength varies with parameter tuning

**Impact:** Current tuning not optimized for self-origin observability

**Mitigation:** Document tuning, explore parameter sensitivity
